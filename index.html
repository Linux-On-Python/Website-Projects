About Me

Hello, I'm Miguel Alejandro. I'm a Software Engineering student transitioning into the creative world of Media Design, with a strong focus on Web Design and UX/UI. My background in software engineering provides me with an analytical and structured problem-solving mindset, as well as technical skills in HTML/CSS and Python. However, my true passion lies in user-centered design: creating intuitive, accessible, and visually compelling digital experiences. I love combining clean code with visual creativity to build interfaces that not only look great but also feel natural to use. I am always eager to learn, adapt to new creative tools, and collaborate on innovative digital projects.
