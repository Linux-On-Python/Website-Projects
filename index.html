About Me

Hi, I’m Miguel Alejandro. I am a Software Engineering student transitioning into the creative world of Media Design, with a strong focus on Web Design and UX/UI. My background in software engineering gives me an analytical, structured problem-solving mindset and technical skills in HTML/CSS and Python. However, my true passion lies in human-centered design—building intuitive, accessible, and visually compelling digital experiences. I love bridging the gap between clean code and visual creativity to build interfaces that not only look great but also feel seamless to the user. I am always eager to learn, adapt to new creative tools, and collaborate on innovative digital projects.
