About Me

Hello, I'm Miguel Alejandro. I am a Software Engineering student transitioning into the creative world of Media Design, with a strong focus on Web Design and UX/UI. My background in software engineering gives me an analytical and structured mindset for problem-solving, as well as technical skills in HTML/CSS and Python. However, my true passion lies in user-centered design: creating intuitive, accessible, and visually appealing digital experiences. I love combining clean code with visual creativity to build interfaces that not only look good but also feel natural to the user. I am always eager to learn, adapt to new creative tools, and collaborate on innovative digital projects.
